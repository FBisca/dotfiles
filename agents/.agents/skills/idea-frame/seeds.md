# Seeds for the idea grilling

Seed the design tree with these branches. Each question becomes a `Q<n>` in `frame.md`. Add branches the answers open; drop none without saying why.

## Heilmeier catechism
1. What are you trying to do? Say it without jargon.
2. How is it done today, and what are the limits of current practice?
3. What is new in your approach, and why might it work?
4. Who has the problem, and who cares? Name a person or a role.
5. If you succeed, what difference does it make?
6. What are the risks?
7. What does it cost?
8. How long will it take?
9. How is success measured mid-way and at the end? Give a number or an observable.

## Internal FAQ (one branch per risk)
- **value**: will the people who have the problem use it, and pay or switch for it?
- **usability**: can they use it unaided, inside their current work?
- **feasibility**: can we build it with the people, data and systems we have?
- **viability**: does it work for the business: cost, compliance, support, timing?
- **ethical**: could it harm someone; would users object if they knew?

For each risk ask: what would have to be true, and what would kill it?

## Story map
After the branches, list the actors (user, system, our team) and the steps each takes to get value. At each step ask: what must be true here? Each answer that is not a lookup becomes an assumption row (the user's belief counts as not a lookup).
