# Interview

Self-contained grilling-style interview. Goal: shared understanding of who the person is, what they want next, and what is true and safe to say publicly.

## Format

Work in **rounds**. Each round asks every question whose prerequisites are settled (the frontier), numbered, each with a recommended answer phrased so "yes" accepts it:

```
❓ **Q1** - **<title>**: <question, with options if useful>

➡️ <recommended answer>
```

- Recommend from what you already read (profile, CV, repos). Never ask for facts you can look up yourself.
- Wait for answers to **every** question. "Proceed" on one item is not approval of the round; re-ask open ones.
- Questions that depend on an unanswered one go to the next round.
- Stop when nothing is left silently assumed; summarize and get explicit "yes" before step 4.

## Question bank (pick what applies; skip what is already known)

**Round 1 — target**
- Target roles/titles (exact strings recruiters search) and seniority.
- Locations: city, remote, relocation, countries with work authorization.
- Industries/domains to lean into vs avoid.
- Actively looking vs passively open? Is the current employer allowed to see it? (sets Open to Work visibility)
- Primary profile language; other languages to support.

**Round 2 — positioning**
- One-line positioning (what they are known for). Offer 2–3 options.
- Top 5 keywords/skills to rank for (must appear in headline, About, Experience).
- Tone for About: first person, plain, no buzzwords; any personal touch (hobbies, values) they want shown.

**Round 3 — experience truth check** (per role, most recent first)
- What they built/owned, for whom, outcome. Real metrics they can stand behind?
- What is confidential (product names, clients, numbers)? → generic wording.
- Side gigs, freelance, advisory, own companies: conflict with current employer? Default: omit.
- Older roles: keep full, condense, or drop?

**Round 4 — extras**
- Projects worth listing (side projects, open source, apps) with links.
- Featured: posts, talks, portfolio, case studies.
- Certifications, education fixes, volunteer.
- Accounts to follow/unfollow (only with explicit yes per account).
- CV wanted? Format (PDF/.docx) and where to save.

## Recording answers
Keep a running "decisions" list in the conversation (not in files inside the current project). Reuse the person's own words where possible.
