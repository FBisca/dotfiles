I want this task seen from more fields than the obvious one, not more opinions. Before proposing anything: (1) restate the task as a verb, what it actually does to someone or something rather than what it is called; (2) name the three to five fields whose professionals do that verb best for a living, at least one far from the obvious. Then propose the crew, one member per field plus one adversary whose job is to break the result (a short seller, forensic accountant, attacker or opposing counsel). For each member give: the role; their one responsibility, meaning the specific thing they own and deliver here; the raw material they work from; the method or tool they would use; and what they hand to whom. The adversary goes last and says what they would attack first. Do the work they do, not how they sound. Propose only; don't start the work or spawn anyone until I say go.
